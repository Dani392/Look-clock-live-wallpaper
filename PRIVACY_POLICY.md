# Privacy Policy for Look Clock

**Last updated:** October 2026

Daniel Salcedo Cias built the Look Clock app as a Free/Freemium application. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Look Clock is an offline-first application designed to respect your privacy. **The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** 

- **Image Processing:** All image cropping, masking, and editing processes are performed locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.
- **Local Storage:** The app saves your custom clock configurations ("Looks") locally on your device's internal storage using standard Android storage mechanisms.

## 2. Permissions Required
To function correctly, the app requests the following permissions:
- **Storage / Media Access:** Required solely to allow you to select background images from your own gallery to use within the app.
- **Set Wallpaper:** Required to apply your created designs as your device's Live Wallpaper.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Look Clock does not integrate any third-party tracking SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks that collect personal user data. 

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
